# Rebel Commandant - Timeline Events Extraction

**Book:** Rebel Commandant by Samuel Hill Charles
**Subject:** Life and rebellion of Muhammad Yusuf Khan (1764)
**Purpose:** Extract historical events with metadata (people, places, dynasties)

---

## Instructions for Timeline Entries
- **Keep descriptions short and crisp** - no flowery language
- **Metadata fields:** People (key figures involved), Places (geographic locations), Dynasties (ruling families/powers involved)
- **Format:** Date | Event | People | Places | Dynasties/Powers

---

## Chapter 1: Introduction

### Key Event
**Date:** 15 October 1764
**Event:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan Bahadur, Governor of Madura and Tinnevelly, is hanged as a rebel in front of the British camp before Madura by order of the Nawab Muhammad Ali Khan.
**People:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan, Muhammad Ali (Nawab of Arcot), Sir John Malcolm (historian)
**Places:** Madura, Tinnevelly, British camp
**Dynasties/Powers:** British East India Company, Nawab of Arcot
**Significance:** Death of one of the ablest Indian soldiers in service to the English

## Chapter 2: Early Career of Yusuf Khan

### Early Life and Conversion
**Date:** circa 1744
**Event:** Yusuf Khan born as Maruthanayagam Pillai, Hindu Vellala caste, in Paniyur.
**People:** Yusuf Khan (birth name Maruthanayagam Pillai), Jacques Law (mentor)
**Places:** Paniyur (Ramnad district)
**Dynasties/Powers:** Vellala caste community

### Religious Conversion and Name Change
**Date:** circa 1750s
**Event:** Maruthanayagam Pillai converts to Islam and adopts name Muhammad Yusuf Khan. Moves to Pondicherry.
**People:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan
**Places:** Pondicherry
**Dynasties/Powers:** French colonial presence in India

### Entry into Service
**Date:** circa 1750s (early)
**Event:** Yusuf Khan enters service under Mr. Brunton in Pondicherry, receives education and language training.
**People:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan, Mr. Brunton
**Places:** Pondicherry
**Dynasties/Powers:** European colonial service

### Service Under Muhammad Kamal
**Date:** circa 1751
**Event:** Yusuf Khan possibly serves as physician to Muhammad Kamal of Nellore, who commands cavalry at siege of Arcot.
**People:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan, Muhammad Kamal, Robert Clive
**Places:** Nellore, Arcot
**Dynasties/Powers:** British East India Company, Nawab of Arcot

### Execution of Muhammad Kamal
**Date:** 1753
**Event:** Muhammad Kamal captured and beheaded by Nawab Muhammad Ali.
**People:** Muhammad Kamal, Nawab Muhammad Ali
**Places:** Nellore district
**Dynasties/Powers:** Nawab of Arcot

### Military Advancement
**Date:** circa 1751-1758
**Event:** Yusuf Khan progressively advances through military ranks: tandalgar, toll-collector, sepoy, naik, havildar, ultimately becoming Subadar (Captain).
**People:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan
**Places:** Nellore region, Carnatic coast
**Dynasties/Powers:** Nawab Muhammad Ali's forces

### Service at Madras Siege
**Date:** 1758-1759
**Event:** Yusuf Khan serves as Vakil (agent) at Madras during siege. Visits Temple of Minakshi at Madura with Muttarughu Pillai.
**People:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan, Muttarughu Pillai
**Places:** Madras, Madura
**Dynasties/Powers:** Nawab Muhammad Ali

### Marriage
**Date:** circa 1762-1763
**Event:** Yusuf Khan marries Portuguese/Pariah woman named Maza. Tandava Raya Pillai (Prime Minister of Ramnad) gifts golden cradle for Yusuf Khan's son.
**People:** Muhammad Yusuf Khan, Maza (wife), Tandava Raya Pillai
**Places:** Arcot or Pondicherry, Ramnad
